Every banner size

Each box below is a slot from the Tecstats ad portal, one for every banner size the portal offers, with its tag pasted in exactly as the portal hands it out. The dashed line marks the size the slot should fill.

An unsold slot shows the Advertise card at the size of the slot. Once a test ad for that size is approved, the ad shows instead. A box that stays empty, or an ad or card that does not fill its box, is what this page is here to catch.
